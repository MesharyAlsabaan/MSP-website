## Editorial rules

1. Every article is written or substantially directed by an MSP professional, and technically reviewed by a second one before publishing.
2. No fabricated findings, statistics, project outcomes or citations. Anything quantitative needs a source a reader can check.
3. Project-based articles describe design intent ("the design aims to…") unless MSP has post-occupancy evidence.
4. AI tools may help with outlining, translation drafts and editing; they are not the author, and their output is never published unreviewed.
5. Both languages are written for their readers, not translated line by line. The Arabic version is reviewed by a native Arabic-speaking professional.
6. Each article links to at least one relevant project or service page, and is linked from it where it adds value.
7. Cadence follows quality: one strong article a month beats four weak ones.

### Outline 1 — Architecture Beyond Buildings
- Opening: a building is judged by people who never read its drawings.
- Different building types, different experiences (residence: comfort, privacy, light; hospitality: arrival, calm, care; commercial: circulation, visibility, vitality).
- Why creativity and performance are not a trade-off.
- Two measures of success: delivery, and the life afterwards.
- How this shapes MSP's briefing questions (examples from the design process, not invented outcomes).
- Close: link to Services and a related project.

### Outline 2 — Privacy, daylight and courtyards in Riyadh housing
- The cultural and climatic case for privacy and inward-looking space.
- Courtyards and walkways in an integrated residential community (Al Munsiyah, described from verified project text).
- Daylight without glare; ventilation; outdoor rooms.
- Design intent vs. what can be measured once occupied (and how MSP would like to measure it).
- Links: Al Munsiyah, private villas.

### Outline 3 — Designing for a Riyadh summer
- Climate facts with cited sources (e.g. national meteorological data), no invented numbers.
- Orientation, shading devices, thermal mass, roof treatment, landscape.
- Trade-offs with façade expression — creativity within climate.
- Checklist for owners commissioning a villa.
- Links: residential projects, Services.

### Outline 4 — One model, many disciplines
- What BIM coordination is, in plain language.
- Typical clashes it catches between architecture, structure and MEP.
- Model validation and QA as routine, not marketing.
- Limits: a model is only as good as the decisions behind it.
- Links: Services.

### Outline 5 — AI in architectural practice
- Current, real uses in MSP's workflow (to be confirmed by the team before writing).
- Planned or experimental uses, clearly labelled as such.
- Why professional judgement and accountability stay human.
- Links: About (technology section).

Outlines 6–10 should be drafted with the responsible professional before any writing starts.

## Workflow

1. Topic and outline agreed with the CEO or a nominated editor.
2. Draft by the subject-matter professional (EN or AR first, whichever is natural).
3. Technical review by a second professional.
4. Localisation into the other language and native review.
5. SEO pass: one primary keyword from the keyword map, SEO title ≤ 60 characters, description ≤ 155 characters, cover image with descriptive filename, related project set.
6. Publish in the admin; the next frontend deploy prerenders it and adds it to the sitemap.
