# Madinah Hospitality Insight — Design Specification

## Purpose

Publish a bilingual MSP insight that presents the Madinah Hotel as a complete hospitality project rooted in the city’s culture of welcome. The restaurant images illustrate one chapter of the hotel experience; they must not redefine the project as a restaurant-only commission.

## Editorial direction

- Arabic title: `فندق المدينة المنورة: تصميم ضيافة يستلهم إرث المكان`
- English title: `Madinah Hotel: Hospitality Design Rooted in Place`
- Category: `Hospitality · ضيافة`
- Author: `MSP Design`
- Mention the Prophetic migration respectfully as historical and cultural context: Madinah’s association with welcome, shelter, and care gives hospitality a special meaning in the city.
- Connect this meaning to calm arrival, privacy, intuitive movement, human-scale interiors, material warmth, lighting, and service efficiency.
- Present the restaurant as one social space within a wider hotel journey. State that lobby, rooms, and other spaces can be added as the visual record grows.
- Do not invent the operator, star rating, area, room count, exact location, completion date, or construction claims.
- Credit the architectural perspective to `المعماري منصور السبعان وفريق MSP` / `architect Mansour Al-Sabaan and the MSP team` once, naturally.

## Experience

- `/blog` remains the bilingual Insights index and each card links to `/blog/:slug`.
- The detail page shows category, date when present, bilingual title and excerpt, cover image, editorial body with section headings, an image gallery/lightbox, and a return link.
- Each article has bilingual SEO title and description fields editable in admin.
- Admin supports multiple article gallery images using the existing upload field.
- Existing article records remain valid when gallery and SEO values are absent.
